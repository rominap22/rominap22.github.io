I'm a Solutions Architect at AWS focused on semiconductor and AI silicon companies like Texas Instruments, Broadcom, and Silicon Labs. I work closely with their engineering teams to scope and design cloud infrastructure for EDA, HPC, and AI/ML workloads.

Day to day, that covers HPC cluster design for chip verification, GPU and custom silicon compute planning, data perimeter design, cloud migrations, and security architecture reviews. I've open-sourced infrastructure-as-code on [aws-samples](https://github.com/aws-samples/aws-networkfirewall-cfn-templates) that's been adopted by 40+ customers and partners, and I participate in AWS's Security Technical Field Community. Across my territory, I've influenced over $3M in annual recurring revenue through technical engagements.

Before this role, I interned at Amazon three times, twice at Amazon Devices (Lab126) building internal developer tools and once on the AWS SA Microsoft Workloads team. I studied Computer Science and Advertising at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign.

I work primarily in Python, Java, and AWS infrastructure, and hold four AWS certifications (ML Specialty, Security Specialty, Developer Associate, and SA Associate).

Outside of my technical work, I'm VP of Business Operations for Asians at Amazon SF Bay Area, coordinating events and promoting community across Bay Area offices. I'm also curious about neuroscience, philosophy, and how advertising shapes the way people think, which is partly why this site exists.
